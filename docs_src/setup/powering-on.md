# Powering on the Mobi

If you have a brand-new Mobi, or need to turn it on and get it out of "deep sleep" mode:

1. Plug in the Mobi charging pad that came with the pump, or any other Qi charging pad.
2. Place the Mobi on the charging pad.
3. If no lights appear, hold down the pump button for about 15 seconds until the lights begin to
   flash.
4. When the pump has turned on, it flashes red LED lights and beeps.
